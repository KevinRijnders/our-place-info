A dashboard for home

A little more organised.
A little more together.

Our Place is a privately hosted family dashboard that brings the household’s plans and everyday tasks onto one screen.

This is an information website. The dashboard itself runs on the household’s own computer and is accessed over its private home network. There is no public dashboard login here.

A shared calendar

Connect a Google account to display selected calendars and manage events through the dashboard.

Everyday essentials

Keep family chores and shopping lists together, ready for a quick check on the kitchen tablet.

Home controls

Use connected Home Assistant devices and view supported Blink camera feeds from the household dashboard.

Family memories

Display uploaded family photos in a slideshow, with files stored on the household’s server.

Google Calendar access

Calendar access is optional and requires the account owner’s consent. Our Place reads the calendar list and events for selected calendars. When an authorised household user chooses to do so, it can create, edit or delete events using Google’s Calendar API.

The public pages do not access Google Calendar or receive dashboard data. Read our privacy policy for how the private dashboard handles data, permissions and account disconnection.

Personal household use

This project is maintained by Kevin Rijnders for personal family use. It is not a public hosted service and is not affiliated with Google, Blink or Home Assistant.
