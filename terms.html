Terms of Service

Last updated: 3 November 2026

These terms describe the use of Our Place (Family Dashboard), maintained by Kevin Rijnders for personal household use. The dashboard is hosted on the household’s own computer and made available to people authorised by its operator. This public website provides information only; it does not provide access to the private dashboard or offer a paid hosted service.

Who may use the dashboard

You may use the private dashboard only with the household operator’s permission. Do not share access with unauthorised people, bypass access restrictions, interfere with connected services or use the dashboard for unlawful purposes. The operator may withdraw access or disable features when necessary to maintain the household’s privacy and systems.

Connected accounts and actions

Connect only accounts and devices you own or have permission to manage. Supply a private iCal address only for a calendar you own or have permission to display. Household users who can access the dashboard may be able to read the configured calendar, operate connected home devices and view configured cameras. The dashboard uses trusted home-network access rather than separate sign-ins for each household member.

The calendar feed is read-only; add, edit and delete events in Google Calendar. Check the device and action before operating home controls. Disconnecting a service does not undo previous actions. Keep passwords, account verification codes and access tokens private.

Your content and privacy

You retain your rights in the photos, calendar entries and other content you provide. Provide only content you have permission to use and share with the household. The dashboard accesses and stores content as needed to provide its features, as described in the privacy policy. Use of the dashboard does not transfer ownership of your content to its maintainer.

Respect other people’s privacy when displaying calendars, uploading photos or using cameras. Do not publish household information, recordings or credentials through the public information repository.

Third-party services

Google Calendar, Home Assistant, Blink, GitHub and any other connected services have their own terms and privacy practices. Their availability, permissions and functionality can change. Our Place is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google, Amazon/Blink, Home Assistant or GitHub. The Blink helper is an unofficial integration and does not provide a Blink subscription or override account and camera limits.

Availability and maintenance

Our Place is a personal project provided without a fee. It may be unavailable during power or network outages, maintenance, account reauthorisation or third-party service failures. No continuous availability, support response time or error-free operation is promised. Features may change or be removed. Do not rely on the dashboard as the sole means of controlling essential devices, receiving urgent reminders or monitoring safety and security.

The household operator is responsible for the host computer, access permissions, software maintenance and backups. Confirm important actions in the underlying service when necessary, and keep independent access to your accounts and devices.

Stopping use and removing data

You may stop using the dashboard at any time. The private calendar feed can be disconnected through Settings on the server laptop. Reset the secret address in Google Calendar to invalidate previous copies. Contact the household operator for removal of other local dashboard data and relevant backups. Details are in the privacy policy.

They describe household use of the running dashboard and do not replace any applicable software licence. The maintainer may update these terms as the dashboard changes; the date above identifies the latest revision.

Contact
